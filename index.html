Hi, I’m Yongkang Chen (陈永康). I am a Research Assistant in AI Metrology at the National Institute of Metrology, China. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Academy of Military Sciences, where I was affiliated with the National Key Laboratory of Science and Technology on Information System Security.

My research goal is to make large language models (LLMs) reliable in open-ended settings, where they must reason, use tools, and collaborate with humans. More broadly, I study how better post-training methods, safer environment design, more rigorous evaluations, and continuous safety monitoring can make LLM risks observable, assessable, and controllable.
