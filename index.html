I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Academy of Military Sciences, where I was affiliated with the National Key Laboratory of Science and Technology on Information System Security.

My research goal is to make large language models (LLMs) reliable in open-ended settings, where they must reason, use tools, and work with people. More broadly, I study how stronger post-training methods, better evaluations, and continuous safety monitoring can make LLM risks observable, assessable, and controllable.
